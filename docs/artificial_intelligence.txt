ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial Intelligence is a technology
that enables computers to perform tasks
that normally require human intelligence.

AI can perform tasks such as:

1. Understanding language
2. Recognizing images
3. Solving problems
4. Making predictions
5. Answering questions

Applications of Artificial Intelligence:

1. Chatbots
2. Virtual assistants
3. Self-driving technology
4. Medical diagnosis support
5. Language translation

AI is used in education, healthcare,
business, transportation and many other fields.
